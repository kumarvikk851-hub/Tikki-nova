Privacy Policy — Tikki Nova

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect basic account information such as your phone number, name, email, or profile photo only when you sign in using Firebase Authentication (Phone OTP, Google, or Facebook).

2. Camera, Microphone & Storage

Camera, microphone, and gallery access are used only when you choose to record videos, go live, or upload photos/videos in the app.

3. Data Security & Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Data and authentication are handled securely via Google Firebase.

4. Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your account anytime inside the app (Profile → Delete My Account) or via our Delete Account Page.
